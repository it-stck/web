# Cookie Policy

**Last updated:** September 29, 2026

## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser to retain technical state, preferences, and session information.

This page outlines how **ITSTCK** (`itstck.com`) utilizes cookies and local storage technologies in compliance with European Union ePrivacy Directive and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / EU 2016/679) guidelines.

---

## Cookies Used on This Website

### 1. Essential Preference Cookies (Strictly Necessary)

These cookies are technically required to operate the site's explicit functionality, such as storing your privacy options. They do not track personal data.

* **Name:** `itstck_consent`
* **Purpose:** Stores your explicit cookie banner acceptance or management choices so the banner does not re-appear on every page request.
* **Provider:** ITSTCK
* **Duration:** 12 months
* **Legal Basis:** Legitimate interest / Strictly Necessary under ePrivacy Directive (art. 5.3). Consent is not required.

---

### 2. Google Analytics (Performance & Analytics — Optional)

These cookies collect aggregated and anonymized statistical information about website traffic, visitor interactions, geographical region, and device performance metrics.

* **Names:** `_ga`, `_ga_*`, `_gid`, `_gat`
* **Purpose:** Analyzes audience interaction to help improve technical content and platform usability. IP anonymization (`anonymizeIp`) is active by default.
* **Provider:** Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited
* **Duration:** Up to 2 years
* **Legal Basis:** Explicit user consent (GDPR Art. 6.1.a).

> **Note:** Analytical scripts **will only execute after you explicitly grant consent** via the consent banner.

---

### 3. Security & Infrastructure Cookies (Strictly Necessary)

* **Names:** `__cf_bm`, `cf_clearance`
* **Purpose:** Distinguishes genuine human visitors from automated botnets, mitigates DDoS attacks, and ensures edge security delivery.
* **Provider:** Cloudflare, Inc.
* **Duration:** Session up to 1 year
* **Legal Basis:** Strictly necessary for cloud security under ePrivacy regulations.

---

## Managing or Revoking Consent

You can revoke your consent at any time by clicking the **Manage Cookies** link in the site footer, or by clearing your browser cache and cookies for `itstck.com`.

Once cleared, the cookie consent banner will automatically reappear upon your next page reload.

---

## Browser-Level Cookie Management

All modern browsers allow you to block or restrict third-party cookies or block cookies entirely. Blocking technical cookies will not break the primary reading experience on `itstck.com`.

* [Google Chrome Settings](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
* [Mozilla Firefox Settings](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/enhanced-tracking-protection-firefox-desktop)
* [Apple Safari Settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471/mac)
* [Microsoft Edge Settings](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-6394125f-421f-b3a5-b00b-77f4f329bc9e)
