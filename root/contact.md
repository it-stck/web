## Communication Guidelines & Response Expectations

To help us triage and respond to your messages efficiently, please keep the following operational standards in mind:

### :material-clock-outline: Response SLAs
* **General Inquiries:** Typically reviewed and answered within **24 to 48 business hours**.
* **Security Disclosures:** Triaged with high priority within **12 hours**. Encrypt sensitive vulnerability data with our public PGP key whenever applicable.

### :material-check-circle-outline: Primary Contact Reasons
* **Documentation Fixes:** Found an outdated command, configuration typo, or broken reference? Let us know or open a Pull Request.
* **Technical Collaboration:** Co-authoring engineering guides, threat research, or benchmarking open-source security tools.
* **Enterprise Consulting:** Inquiries regarding custom zero-trust deployment, Cloud/DevOps architecture advisory, or system auditing.

### :material-block-helper: Spam & Automated Outreach
> **Notice:** Unsolicited commercial offers, automated sales pitches, link-building outreach, and generic marketing proposals are automatically filtered and permanently discarded.
